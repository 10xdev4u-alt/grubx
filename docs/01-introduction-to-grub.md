# What is GRUB?

**GRUB** stands for **GRand Unified Bootloader**.

## In Simple Terms
Imagine your computer is a restaurant.
- The **Operating System (OS)** (like Linux or Windows) is the food.
- **GRUB** is the waiter who hands you the menu as soon as you sit down.

Without GRUB, your computer wouldn't know which "dish" (OS) to serve you.

## Why Do We Theme It?
By default, GRUB looks like a boring text terminal from the 1980s.
**PrinceTheme** turns that boring menu into a 5-star dining experience.

## Key Components
- **Kernel:** The core of the OS that GRUB loads.
- **Initrd:** Temporary file system used during boot.
- **Theme:** The visual layer (images, fonts) that sits on top of the logic.

## Why "Grand Unified"?
Before GRUB, there were many bootloaders (LILO, etc.). GRUB aimed to unify them all into one powerful standard that could boot almost anything.
